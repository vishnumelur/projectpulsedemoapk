Batch 1, revised: 4 taps from opening the app to Home

No forms. The role choice now lives on the Welcome screen. Sign up is just two fields. Choosing a building type or stage moves on automatically, with no "Continue" button. And Pulse asks what you need in conversation on Home, instead of making you fill in a text form. There's one button colour everywhere (Pulse Blue), the emoji are replaced by custom line-art drawings, and the motion uses slow, soft easing.

Where the old "Describe your need" form went

It's gone. On first arrival at Home, Pulse greets you: "Hi Sara 👋 Your villa is at the Tender stage. What do you need help with?", with tappable suggestions such as "Review contractor bids", "Book a site visit" and "Cost check". You'll see this in Batch 2.
